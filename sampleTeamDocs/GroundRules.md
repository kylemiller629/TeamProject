
# Team Ground Rules

## 1. Branching Strategy and Code Reviews
- Each team member will create their own branch from `main`
  to work on assigned features.
- Team members will commit and push their changes regularly.
- All changes to `main` must go through a pull request.
- At least one other team member must review and approve
  a pull request before it is merged.
- Team members will resolve merge conflicts and address
  feedback before merging changes.
- The `main` branch should contain working, tested code.

## 2. Team Meetings
- The team will meet at least once a week for a minimum
  of one hour.
- Meetings will be used to discuss progress, challenges,
  upcoming tasks, and project deadlines.
- Additional meetings may be scheduled if needed.
- Team members will notify the group if they cannot
  attend a scheduled meeting.

## 3. Communication
- Team members will communicate through a group chat.
- Questions, concerns, and project updates should be
  shared with the group.
- Team members will respond to messages in a reasonable
  amount of time.
- Any blockers or delays should be communicated as
  soon as possible.

## 4. Asking for Help
- Team members are encouraged to ask for help when needed.
- Everyone will make an effort to help teammates when
  they are available.
- Problems should be addressed early rather than waiting
  until deadlines approach.
- Team members will share useful resources and findings.

## 5. Accountability
- Each team member is responsible for completing their
  assigned tasks by the agreed-upon deadlines.
- If a team member cannot complete a task on time, they
  should notify the group as soon as possible.
- If work is not completed, the team will contact the
  individual to discuss the situation and determine
  the next steps.
- If a team member becomes unresponsive or repeatedly
  fails to contribute, the team will contact Paula
  for guidance.

## 6. Understanding the Project
- Development tasks will be divided among team members
  to maintain a balanced workload.
- Each team member will explain their contributions
  during team meetings.
- Team members will review each other's code to gain
  an understanding of all parts of the application.
- Everyone should be able to explain the overall
  architecture and functionality of the REST API.
- All team members will participate in preparing
  for the final presentation.

## 7. Respect and Collaboration
- Team members will treat each other with respect.
- Everyone will have an opportunity to share ideas
  and opinions.
- Feedback should be constructive and professional.
- Team members will remain open to different approaches
  and suggestions.

## 8. Decision Making and Conflict Resolution
- Major project decisions will be discussed as a team.
- The team will attempt to reach a consensus.
- If an agreement cannot be reached, decisions will
  be made by majority vote.
- Conflicts will be addressed respectfully and directly.
- If the team cannot resolve a conflict, Paula may
  be contacted for guidance.

## 9. Workload and Deadlines
- Work will be distributed as evenly as possible
  based on each member's skills and availability.
- Tasks and deadlines will be tracked using GitHub Issues.
- Team members will update the status of their assigned
  tasks regularly.
- Everyone will contribute to coding, testing,
  documentation, and the final presentation.

## 10. Documentation and Progress
- Team members will document their work through
  meaningful Git commits and GitHub Issues.
- The team will maintain a weekly progress log
  documenting accomplishments, challenges, and lessons.
- Code should be readable, organized, and documented.
- The team will review instructor feedback and make
  necessary improvements before final submission.

If you aren't sure what to include, search for "ground rules for software development teams".  Be sure to include items like: 

1. Branching strategy, code review and pull request strategy - consider creating a [contribution guidelines document](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/setting-up-your-project-for-healthy-contributions/setting-guidelines-for-repository-contributors).
2. How will your team meet? MS Teams, Discord? How often?
3. How will your team communicate between meetings? How often?
4. What are the expectations when a team member needs help?
5. What happens if a team member does not complete work they agreed to? What if a team member disappears? 
6. How will you ensure all team members can demonstrate full understanding of all parts of the application?
7. What else is important to YOU to include? Accountability? Respect? How are decisions made? How are conflicts resolved?  Balanced workload?
